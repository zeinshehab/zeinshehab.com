SpaceFood is a full-stack social platform for discovering and sharing recipes, developed as a team project for the Software Engineering course at AUB.

I led the team in building the platform end-to-end, including:

- **Authentication and role-based access control** for different types of users.
- **Customizable user profiles** for personalization.
- **Recipe publishing** with rich content and categorization.
- **Content feeds** for browsing and discovering new recipes.
- **Tag-based search** for finding recipes by ingredients, cuisine, or category.
- **Social features** including likes, comments, and ratings.

The platform is built with a **React** frontend, a **Node.js** backend exposing REST APIs, and **MongoDB** for data storage.
